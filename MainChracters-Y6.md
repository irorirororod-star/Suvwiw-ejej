## I. Identity & Background

**Au Aragi** and **Lae Aragi** are eighteen-year-old fraternal twins born on March 14 during Pi Minute — Au at 3:14 AM, seven minutes before Lae at 3:21 AM. They reside together with their parents at 47 Elmswood Terrace in Everl, the capital city of the world of Everleigh. Both are high school juniors: Au focuses on Political Science and Sociology and serves on the Student Council, while Lae pursues Psychology and Philosophy. The family home functions as both fortress and sanctuary — a private world where the walls keep the rest of reality out and their secrets in. Lae constantly wishes for more walls and thicker doors.

Au is known as "The Four-Armed Silent Storm" and serves as "The Heart of His World." Lae is "The Quiet Anchor" and "The Core of Her World." Together, they form a closed, self-sustaining system — two halves of a dyad that needs nothing and no one else to be complete. Their love evolved naturally from siblinghood into a profound, all-consuming, unconventional romantic bond. They are both fully aware of its nature. They both accept it without shame or doubt. They both chose it. They hide it from the world because exposure would mean separation, and separation is not survivable for either of them.
